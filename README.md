A complete story in exactly six words: a woman forgives the man who wronged her — only to find forgiveness came too late. The arc is a whole life in miniature: a wrong, years of withholding, a change of heart, and a door already closed. What he did, and what "too late" means, is left for the reader to fill in.

This entry's program (`main.ts`, Node 22, stdlib only) writes that single six-word story to `/output/entry.txt`, after checking it is exactly six words and 1–60 characters.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
